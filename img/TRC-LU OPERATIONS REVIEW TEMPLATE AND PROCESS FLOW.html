FACILITATOR’S GUIDE FOR OPERATIONS REVIEW
This guide is designed to help facilitators conduct an efficient and meaningful
Operations Review. It provides a step-by-step flow to ensure that all essential topics
are covered and that the session leads to actionable results.
Step 1: Opening and Orientation
• Welcome participants and state the purpose of the Operations Review.
• Remind everyone that the session aims to assess progress, address
challenges, and plan actionable steps.
Step 2: Basic Information
• Confirm Department/Division, Head/Deputy, and assigned Documenter.
• Ensure attendance of key staff and clarify roles during the session.

Step 3: Strategic Contributions
• Review contributions/tasks aligned with the strategic plan.
• Discuss deadlines, status updates, and challenges faced.
• Identify tasks that need follow-up or support.
Step 4: Operations Review
• Confirm meeting venue, schedule, scoreboard location, and OIC.
• Present operational updates (using scoreboards or reports).
• Discuss key issues, gaps, and challenges.
• Fill out the Action Points table, assigning responsibilities, target dates, and
status.
Step 5: Recognition
• Discuss how wins and milestones will be celebrated.
• Identify ways to recognize best performers formally or informally.
• Agree on the frequency of recognition to maintain morale and motivation.
Step 6: Closing
• Summarize key decisions and action points.
• Remind responsible persons of their commitments.
• End with a motivational message that reinforces teamwork and accountability.
Tips for Facilitators
• Keep discussions focused and time-bound.
• Encourage open sharing but redirect if the conversation goes off-topic.
• Ensure action points are specific, measurable, and assigned to the right
people.
• Document key outputs clearly for accountability.
